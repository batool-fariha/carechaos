# Code of conduct

We want CareChaos to be a welcoming project. Contributors and maintainers agree to:

* Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
* Keep discussion constructive and on topic. No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention.
* Respect privacy. Never post real patient data, credentials or other people's personal information.
* Give and accept feedback graciously.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules and may ban repeat offenders. To report a problem, open a private security advisory or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This is a short adaptation of the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
